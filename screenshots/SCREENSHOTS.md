# Screenshots

Add screenshots here as you capture them:

- `main-view.png` — the shortener with a freshly shortened link and its QR code.
- `history.png` — the history list with several links and click counts.
- `mobile-view.png` — the layout on a phone-sized viewport.

To capture: open `src/index.html` in a browser, shorten a link, and take a
screenshot (or use the browser's device toolbar for the mobile shot).
